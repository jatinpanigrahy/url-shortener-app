About URL Shortener

A clean, dependable link utility designed for simplicity and control.

Core Capabilities

Meaningful Custom Aliases

Assign recognizable names to your links so they are easy to share, read, and remember across channels.

High-Resolution QR Codes

Generate clean QR codes alongside short links and export them as PNGs for slides, documents, or print materials.

Scheduled Link Expiration

Configure time-limited links for seasonal promotions or temporary resources, preventing expired content from lingering.

Organized Link History

Keep track of past links, review click counts, and export your history to CSV whenever you need a backup or report.

Direct Experience

Zero Wait Timers or Ads

Visitors are redirected immediately without intermediate landing pages, countdown timers, or advertising screens.

Responsive Interface

Designed for fast and clear interaction across mobile devices, tablets, and desktop browsers alike.

Privacy & Ownership

No Tracking Profiles

We do not profile visitor browsing behavior, insert advertising trackers, or share redirect traffic data.

Full Data Control

Manage your links anytime. Deleting an entry removes it from active redirection immediately.

Open Source Foundation

The entire project is open source and available on GitHub for auditability and self-hosting.
