Core Capabilities

Meaningful Custom Aliases

Assign recognizable names to your links so they are easy to share, read, and remember across channels.

High-Resolution QR Codes

Generate clean QR codes alongside short links and export them as PNGs for slides, documents, or print materials.

Scheduled Link Expiration

Configure time-limited links for seasonal promotions or temporary resources, preventing expired content from lingering.

Organized Link History

Keep track of past links, review click counts, and export your history to CSV whenever you need a backup or report.

Responsive Interface
